# Pramanik — Agents and residents edition

110 seconds · 1280 × 720 · 30 fps · 3,300 frames. No audio track.

The approved revised wording identifies agents as the actors doing casework and uses “we” for residents. A new silent voice-conversation waveform uses deterministic frame-driven SVG bars. The larger follow-up cycle, animated dusty complaint box, focused issue map, Fog Rise introduction and final wordmark remain.

## Exact on-screen copy

### 0–3s

India has a
follow-up problem.

### 3–6s

We pay taxes.
We deserve answers.

### 6–12s

Our complaints get logged.
But we’re still left chasing repairs.

### 12–17s

And we don’t always have time
to keep calling, visiting offices,
and asking for updates.

### 17–21s

Introducing Pramanik.

### 21–26s

Pramanik’s agents keep following up,
so we don’t have to
do all the chasing.

### 26–33s

The agents combine repeated complaints
about the same problem into one shared case.

### 33–37s

Our reports, photos, locations,
and earlier conversations
give the agents the full picture.

### 37–46s

Specialist agents review the complaints,
check the history, and identify who is responsible.

A coordinator agent brings their findings together into an action plan.

Powered by OpenAI Agents SDK · GPT-5.4 mini

### 46–51s

The agents search public ward directories
to find the responsible local officer
or representative.

### 51–58s

Then the voice agents call that person
in Hindi, Marathi, or English.

Powered by GPT-Realtime-2.1

Bolna gives the agents a real phone number to make calls.

### 58–64s

The agents remember previous conversations.
They ask what happened to earlier promises—
and what will happen next.

Powered by GPT-5.4 mini · Case memory + reasoning

### 64–70s

The agents turn each promise
into a recorded commitment,
a deadline, and a
scheduled follow-up.

Powered by Tool calling + persistent background jobs

### 70–77s

When an officer says the work is done,
the agents ask us to check what actually happened.

Powered by GPT visual assessment + human review

### 77–82s

We can respond to a public survey,
share photos, and report
what we see on the ground.

### 82–86s

The agents compare that evidence
with the officer’s claim.

### 86–92s

If the evidence disagrees,
the agents reopen the case and call again—
asking for an explanation and the next action.

### 92–97s

The agents publish conversation records,
promises, evidence, and next steps.

### 97–102s

So we can inspect the progress,
question the answers,
and verify the outcome together.

### 102–107s

We report the problem.
Pramanik’s agents keep following up.

### 107–110s

Pramanik.
