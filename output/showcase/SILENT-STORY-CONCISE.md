# Pramanik — Concise Lavender film

101 seconds · 1280 × 720 · 30 fps · 3,030 frames. No audio track.

All narrative beats are two lines at most. Residents are addressed as “you”; agents are named as the actors doing casework. The Lavender mesh runs continuously across all scenes. The archive uses precise isometric geometry, neutral material shading, file stacks, an inset handle and an angled label.

## Exact copy

### 0–3s

India has a
follow-up problem.

### 3–6s

You pay taxes.
Public servants owe you answers.

### 6–13s

Your complaint gets logged.
You’re still chasing repairs.

### 13–17s

You shouldn’t have to keep calling
and visiting offices for answers.

### 17–21s

Introducing Pramanik.

### 21–25s

Pramanik’s agents follow up,
so you don’t have to.

### 25–32s

Agents remove duplicate complaints
and group related reports into one case.

### 32–36s

Reports, photos, location and past calls
give agents the full picture.

### 36–44s

Specialist agents check reports,
history and who’s responsible.

A coordinator agent turns their findings into an action plan.

Powered by OpenAI Agents SDK · GPT-5.4 mini

### 44–48s

Agents find officers’ and leaders’ contacts
in public ward directories.

### 48–55s

Voice agents call the person responsible
in Hindi, Marathi or English.

Powered by GPT-Realtime-2.1

Bolna gives the agents a real phone number to make calls.

### 55–59s

Agents recall past calls and promises,
then ask what’s done—and what’s next.

Powered by GPT-5.4 mini · Case memory + reasoning

### 59–65s

Agents save promises and deadlines,
then schedule a follow-up.

Powered by Tool calling + persistent background jobs

### 65–72s

An officer says “done”.
Agents ask you to check.

Powered by GPT visual assessment + human review

### 72–76s

You share survey responses, photos
and observations from the ground.

### 76–80s

Agents compare your evidence
with the officer’s claim.

### 80–85s

Conflicting evidence reopens the case.
Agents call again for answers and action.

### 85–90s

Agents publish calls, promises and evidence.
A public dashboard shows progress and next steps.

### 90–94s

You inspect progress, question answers
and verify the outcome together.

### 94–98s

You report the problem.
Pramanik’s agents keep following up.

### 98–101s

Pramanik.
